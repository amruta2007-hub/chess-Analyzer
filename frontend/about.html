About Chess Analyzer

Transparent, local, engine-driven chess game analysis with zero subscriptions or paywalls.

Our Mission

Chess Analyzer is designed for players who want to understand their mistakes, master key strategic concepts, and explore alternative continuations without subscription fees, forced accounts, or privacy concerns.

All evaluations are generated directly by your configured Stockfish chess engine via the standard UCI protocol. No user games are permanently stored in any database.

Move Classification Methodology

Moves are classified based on the centipawn evaluation difference (Δ) between the engine's recommended best move and the move actually played on the board, evaluated strictly from the perspective of the player whose turn it was:

!! Brilliant
Tactical Sacrifice with Winning Advantage
A move matching the engine's best choice that sacrifices piece material while establishing or maintaining a decisive winning advantage.
★ Best
Top Engine Choice (Δ ≤ 12 cp)
The engine's highest-rated continuation, maintaining optimal dynamic pressure.
✓ Excellent
Strong Move (12 < Δ ≤ 35 cp)
An effective, solid choice that preserves the fundamental evaluation without major concession.
● Good
Solid Play (35 < Δ ≤ 75 cp)
Playable move with slight loss in engine dynamism or initiative.
?! Inaccuracy
Sub-Optimal (75 < Δ ≤ 150 cp)
Gives away a noticeable fraction of advantage or hands initiative to the opponent.
? Mistake
Notable Error (150 < Δ ≤ 300 cp)
Significantly worsens the position or squanders a winning advantage.
?? Blunder
Major Blunder (Δ > 300 cp or Mate Blunder)
A game-changing mistake that drops critical material or allows a forced checkmate.

Estimated Accuracy Calculation

Accuracy is estimated using the standard logistic win-probability function:

WinProbability(cp) = 100 / (1 + exp(-0.00368208 * cp))
Accuracy(move) = 103.1668 * exp(-0.04354 * WinProbLoss) - 3.1669

Disclaimer: Estimated accuracy is an open-source mathematical calculation based on win probability decay. It is not the proprietary Chess.com CAPS score.

Stockfish Engine Configuration

On Render Deployment: The included Dockerfile automatically installs the official Stockfish engine package via Debian apt and configures STOCKFISH_PATH=/usr/games/stockfish. No manual setup is required on Render.

On Local Windows Development: A pre-configured Stockfish engine is placed in bin/stockfish.exe. If updating:

  1. Download the official binary from stockfishchess.org/download/.
  2. Rename the executable to stockfish.exe.
  3. Place it in chess-analyzer/bin/stockfish.exe.
